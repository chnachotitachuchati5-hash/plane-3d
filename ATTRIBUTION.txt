Noy Passenger Jet — 3D aircraft demo
====================================

Built as a self-contained static site. No accounts, no deployment.

Libraries (vendored in ./vendor and also loadable from a CDN):
- Three.js r170  — MIT License  https://threejs.org/
- GSAP 3.12.7    — Standard "no charge" license (GreenSock)
- Kanit font     — SIL Open Font License, via Google Fonts

The airplane is original procedural geometry built from Three.js
primitives (no third-party 3D models, no downloaded meshes).
The "HS-NOY" livery is fictional and is not a real airline or
aircraft type. Proportions are loosely inspired by modern
twin-engine passenger jets in general, not a copy of any
specific airframe.
